Privacy Policy

English Naya — MACONE Tecnologia

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how English Naya handles information when you use its English lessons, music activities, translations, speech playback and optional premium purchase.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores your nickname, selected language, voice preference, display theme, learning progress and cached translations locally on your device. It also stores purchase verification information locally to maintain premium access.

English Naya does not require an app account. MACONE Tecnologia does not operate a server that receives your nickname or learning progress. Automatic Android backup is disabled for the app.

2. Translation and Google ML Kit

Some translations are included with the app. Other lesson translations use Google ML Kit on-device translation. Downloading a language model initially requires an internet connection over Wi-Fi. After the required model is available, translation processing takes place on your device.

ML Kit does not send the input text or translated output to Google's servers for translation processing.

Google ML Kit collects technical information for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance and service improvement. This includes device and application information, installation or other technical identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, events, error codes and configured translation languages. Translation also uses Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations for remote configuration and diagnostics.

These services communicate with Google to download models, obtain configuration updates and transmit technical metrics. Google states that ML Kit's disclosed collected data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Further information: ML Kit Terms and Privacy , ML Kit data disclosures and Firebase Privacy and Security .

3. Speech playback

Spoken lessons use the Android text-to-speech service and the voice selected on your device. An online voice may send the text being read and related technical data to its service provider. An installed offline voice can process supported speech locally.

The selected speech provider's privacy policy applies to its processing. English Naya does not record your voice and does not request microphone access.

4. Purchases through Google Play

Optional premium access is provided through a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app receives purchase information, such as the product identifier, purchase token, signed purchase record and purchase status, to verify the purchase, unlock content and restore access.

MACONE Tecnologia does not receive your payment card details. Payment processing, Google account information and Google's transaction records are handled by Google under its policies. Restoring a purchase requires the Google Play account used for the original purchase.

See Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising and permissions

English Naya does not display advertisements or integrate advertising networks. It does not use the Android advertising identifier for advertising or tracking.

Internet access and network status are used for language model downloads, Google service communications and purchase verification. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

6. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and progress remain on your device until you reset them or remove the app's data. You can reset learning progress in the app's settings. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings, select English Naya, open its storage options and clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Clearing local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase or delete records retained by Google or your speech provider. Downloaded language models may be managed by Google services. Third-party data retention and deletion follow the respective providers' policies.

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request, retaining correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations.

7. Security

Local information is protected by Android's app storage controls. Purchase records are verified before premium access is granted. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

English Naya is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, please contact us so we can review the request and delete information where applicable.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version and effective date will be displayed on this page.
